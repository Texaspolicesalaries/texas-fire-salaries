For departments

Claim your department page

Departments can claim their profile to maintain compensation information directly and earn a ◆ Department maintained badge. The community can still submit corrections.

How claiming works

  1. Select your department.
  2. Provide an official work email (government or department domain).
  3. We compare the email domain against the department or city website.
  4. Exact matches on an approved domain may be approved automatically; unclear claims go to an administrator review queue.

What department representatives can do

  • Maintain salary information and upload official pay plans.
  • Add recruiter contacts and hiring links, and update hiring status.
  • Correct department details and view basic page analytics.
ℹ
A department-maintained page is the only place we use “maintained” status — and community members can still submit disputes or proposed corrections, which remain visible.

Claiming requires a connected Firebase project. Once enabled, start a claim from any department page. Questions? See our Terms and Privacy Policy.
